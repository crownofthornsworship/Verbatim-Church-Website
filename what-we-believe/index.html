Statement of faith

WHAT WE
BELIEVE.

These are the beliefs formally adopted by Verbatim Church. Tap any statement to read it and see the supporting Scriptures.

1. The Bible

We believe the Bible, consisting of the 66 books of the Old and New Testaments, is the inspired, infallible, and authoritative Word of God. It is without error in the original manuscripts and is the final authority in all matters of faith and practice.

2 Timothy 3:16–17; 2 Peter 1:20–21; Psalm 19:7–11; John 17:17; Matthew 5:18

2. The Triune God

We believe in one God, eternally existing in three persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit—equal in power and glory. This one true God is the Creator of all, sovereign over all, and worthy of all worship.

Deuteronomy 6:4; Matthew 28:19; 2 Corinthians 13:14; Genesis 1:1; Isaiah 46:9–10; Revelation 4:11

3. God the Father

We believe in God the Father—holy, loving, just, and all-powerful—who reigns over all creation and providentially cares for His children. He is the source of salvation and adopts believers into His family.

John 3:16; Ephesians 1:3–6; 1 Peter 1:2–3; Romans 8:15–16; Galatians 4:6

4. Jesus Christ

We believe in the full deity and full humanity of Jesus Christ, God’s only begotten Son. He was conceived by the Holy Spirit and born of the Virgin Mary, lived a sinless life, performed miracles, died as a substitutionary atonement for our sins, rose bodily from the dead, ascended to heaven, and now intercedes for believers. He will return personally and visibly in glory.

John 1:1, 14; Colossians 2:9; Matthew 1:18–25; Hebrews 4:15; Isaiah 53:4–6; 1 Corinthians 15:3–8; Revelation 19:11–16

5. The Holy Spirit

We believe in the Holy Spirit, who convicts the world of sin, righteousness, and judgment. He regenerates, indwells, seals, empowers, sanctifies, and teaches believers. He distributes spiritual gifts for the building up of the Church.

John 16:8–11; Titus 3:5; 1 Corinthians 6:19; Ephesians 1:13; Acts 1:8; John 14:26

6. Salvation

We believe that salvation is by grace alone, through faith alone, in Christ alone. All people are sinners by nature and choice and are under just condemnation. Salvation includes regeneration, justification, sanctification, and glorification.

Ephesians 2:8–9; Romans 5:1; Romans 3:10–23; Romans 6:23; John 3:3–5; Romans 8:30

7. The Church

We believe in the universal Church, composed of all true believers in Jesus Christ, and in the local church as the visible expression of that body. The local church exists to glorify God through worship, discipleship, fellowship, and mission.

Ephesians 4:4–6; Acts 2:42–47; John 4:24; Matthew 28:18–20; Hebrews 10:24–25

8. The Ordinances

We believe the Lord Jesus instituted two ordinances for the Church: baptism by immersion—an outward declaration of inward faith—and the Lord’s Supper—a remembrance of Christ’s sacrifice and a proclamation of His return.

Matthew 28:19; Acts 8:36–38; 1 Corinthians 11:23–26

9. The Return of Christ

We believe in the personal, visible, pre-millennial return of Jesus Christ to establish His kingdom on earth. This blessed hope motivates holy living and faithful service.

1 Thessalonians 4:13–18; Revelation 20:1–6; Titus 2:13; 2 Peter 3:11–14

10. Eternity

We believe in the resurrection of the body and eternal judgment. Believers will experience everlasting life with God, while the unrepentant will face eternal separation from Him.

John 5:28–29; Revelation 21:1–7; Matthew 25:46; 2 Thessalonians 1:8–9

11. Marriage and Family

We believe that marriage is a covenant union between one biological man and one biological woman, ordained by God as the foundation of human society. Parents are to raise children in the training and instruction of the Lord.

Genesis 2:24; Matthew 19:4–6; Ephesians 6:4

12. Calvary Chapel Distinctives

We affirm the Calvary Chapel distinctives, including teaching the entire Word of God verse by verse; dependence on the Holy Spirit; a culture of grace and servant leadership; unity in essentials, liberty in non-essentials, and love in all things; and living in anticipation of Christ’s return.

Acts 20:27; 2 Timothy 4:2; Zechariah 4:6; John 13:12–17; Romans 14:5; 1 Corinthians 13:1–7

QUESTIONS ARE
WELCOME.

If you want to know more about what we believe or how we teach the Bible, we would be glad to talk with you.
